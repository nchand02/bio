    How to read this map

    Accuracy. Operational and under-construction stations are plotted at their approximate real positions (within a few hundred metres). For the Orange, Grey and Red lines and the Phase 4 proposals, only key stations are shown and the route is indicative, because final station lists and alignments are still being changed.

    Background. The map has no street tiles because this page cannot load external map images. Outer Ring Road, NICE Road (approximate), main lakes and parks are drawn for orientation.

    Timeline. Past dates are the dates public service started. Future dates are the latest official targets reported as of Nov 2026; "projected" lengths assume those targets are met. The km counter adds section lengths, adjusted so it matches the official totals (73.81 km in Oct 2023, 76.95 km in Nov 2024, 96.1 km in Aug 2025, 103.6 km from 14 Nov 2026). Future totals can differ by 1–2 km from official figures. Interchange stations are counted once.

    Pink Line. PM inauguration of the elevated section is scheduled for 13 Nov 2026 (about 12:45 PM) and public service starts at 6 am on 14 Nov 2026. The map's “Now” view counts it as open.
